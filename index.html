Daily Danagram

Drag a piece, or tap it then tap a destination. Double-tap empty space to undo, triple-tap to redo.

How to play

  • W O R D ✓ Unscramble tiles to make wordsDrag a piece, or tap it and tell it where to go!
  • Word bank New words charge up your word bankEvery new word you make, and every new spot where two words cross, fills the bar on your word bank.
  • Grid Check WORD Grid Check to sort your wordsWhen the bar is full, open the word bank and tap Grid Check to see which words on your board are in today’s puzzle…and which aren’t!
Fill the grid with words, and win!

Tools & Tips

  • Undo/Redo Move back and forth through your history of moves.
  • Shuffle Try out different board arrangements. A great way to find new words and get unstuck.
  • Scatter Move the single letter tiles off to the edges. Sometimes, the best way to solve a Danagram is to start with arranging the larger Ominos.
  • Cross words Every new word charges your word bank, and so does every new spot where two words cross. Building crosswords charges it fastest.

Gestures

  • Drag or tap Drag a piece to move it, or tap a piece and then tap where it should go. Other pieces slide aside to make room.
  • Swipe Swipe on an empty spot of the board to slide every tile that way. Keep going and the tiles wrap around.
  • 2× Double-tap Double-tap an empty spot of the board to undo your last move.
  • 3× Triple-tap Triple-tap an empty spot to redo it.
